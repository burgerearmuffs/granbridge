# GRANBRIDGE v0.1.7 — Dual cameras, big-hit announcements, sharper look

## New

- **📷📷 Dual cameras per player** — add a second camera pointed at your dartboard
  (Settings → *Board camera*). Your opponent sees both your face cam **and** your live board
  during online matches; the in-game rail and the lobby show every feed.
- **🎬 Big-hit announcements** — Treble Twenty, Treble Nineteen, Treble Eighteen, Bullseye and
  **One Eighty** now flash across the screen in broadcast gold. Want real video stingers? Drop
  `treble-twenty.mp4`, `one-eighty.mp4`, `bullseye.mp4`… into the app's `videos/` folder and they
  play instead (same drop-a-file system as the GAME SHOT clips). Honors the reduced-motion and
  video-off settings.

## Improved

- A sharper look across the app: arena-vignette backdrop, gradient brand title, glowing active
  tab, richer camera tiles, gradient win banners with a pop, and a glassier in-match side rail.
