# GRANBRIDGE v0.1.8 — Profiles, match history & rivalries

## New

- **📜 Match history on your Profile** — your recent games now sync to the server and show up on
  the Profile page (opponent, mode, win/loss, 3-dart average). Keyed to your player ID, so it
  follows you across devices when you restore your recovery key.
- **⚔️ Head-to-head records** — the pre-match opponent card now shows your rivalry line
  (e.g. *vs you: 4–2*) against the player you're about to face.
- **✍️ Profile bio** — add a short bio to your profile (Profile → *Bio*). Your name, avatar colour
  and bio now sync to the server **without** having to finish a match first.

## Notes

These features light up when your broker runs the matching server build (**server-v0.4.0**+). If the
server is older or unreachable, the app falls back gracefully — history hides, stats stay local, and
nothing breaks.
